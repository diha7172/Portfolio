Colorado Design Build Fly is CU Boulder's student-run team for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition, and I'm its Testing Lead. The team needed one public home where a first-year with no RC experience or a potential corporate partner could understand who we are in a minute. I designed and built coloradodbf.org as that single source of truth: recruiting, sponsorship, the competition record, events, and member resources, all in one place.

  • Built a nine-page static site — Home, About, Team, Competition, Events, Sponsorship, Contact, Join, and a member Resources page — on the coloradodbf.org custom domain, with cache-busted assets and a mobile drawer navigation.
  • Led the home page with a live countdown to the 31st AIAA fly-off (Tucson, 15 April 2027) and two clear calls to action, Join the team and Partner with us, so every visitor has an obvious next step.
  • Profiled all eleven student leads and the faculty advisor by role on the Team page, and broke the aircraft down by subteam on the About page — Aerodynamics, Structures & Mission, Avionics & Telemetry, Manufacturing, and Finance & Outreach — so recruits can find where they fit.
  • Documented the competition: how a DBF season runs from the August rules release to the April fly-off, plus the team's full record since 2003, including a 3rd-place overall finish at Wichita in 2012 and top-five finishes in 2008, 2012, and 2013.
  • Wrote the Sponsorship page to position support as an engineering investment and built a showcase of 13+ partners — Boom Supersonic, Atlas Motion Systems, the Engineering Excellence Fund, Ansys, SolidWorks, Xometry, SendCutSend, and more.
  • Set an editorial design system — Bricolage Grotesque display type, Inter body copy, and JetBrains Mono labels over full-bleed team photography — and wove the "built a mile up, at 5,430 ft" story through the copy as the team's differentiator.
