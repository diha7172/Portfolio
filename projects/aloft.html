Aloft — Sign-inThe Aloft landing screen. Warm paper-and-ink design language; flights are private to each account.
Full-Stack Developer & Designer

Aloft is a personal flight logging web app I designed and built end-to-end: every flight I take is logged, mapped, and remembered. Type in an airline, flight number, and date, and the app auto-fills the rest — airports, terminals, gates, aircraft registration, scheduled versus actual times — via a live flight-data lookup. Flights render on an interactive world map, and the whole log is private to each account through Postgres Row-Level Security.

  • Designed a 48-field PostgreSQL schema capturing the complete flight record: departure/arrival airports with IATA/ICAO codes, coordinates and timezones, scheduled/estimated/actual times, aircraft registration and type, seat, cabin, and route distance.
  • Built a serverless flight-lookup Edge Function (TypeScript/Deno on Supabase) that auto-fills flight details from a live flight-data API, so logging a flight takes seconds instead of a form-filling session.
  • Rendered flight routes on an interactive Leaflet world map — 98 flights logged and mapped to date.
  • Enforced per-user privacy with Postgres Row-Level Security: every row is scoped to the owning account at the database layer, not just the UI.
  • Shipped as a single-page app deployed on Vercel with Supabase handling auth, database, and serverless functions.
