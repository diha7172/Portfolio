All 4 unique Chicago spots — no chains
Gene & Jude's 🌭
Near hotel
📍 2720 N River Rd, River Grove (5 min from hotel)
The iconic Chicago hot dog counter open since 1945. Cash only. No ketchup, no tomato, no relish. Pure Chicago dog. The fresh-cut fries are legendary. 30-min walk from hotel on flat roads if you feel like stretching on arrival.
Order: Double dog + fries. Bring cash. Do not ask for ketchup.

The Purple Pig 🐷
Day 1 · Dinner

Day 2 · Lunch

Pequod's Pizza 🍕
Day 2 · Dinner
📍 2207 N Clybourn Ave, Lincoln Park
The most distinct deep dish in Chicago. The caramelized cheese crust is a completely different experience — the edge of the pan gets coated in cheese that crisps into a crackling ring. Cult status among locals.
Order: Sausage + any veggie, caramelized crust. Put your name in immediately. 30-min wait. Eat the edges. ~$20–25/person.

Mr. Beef 🥩
Day 3 · Last meal
📍 666 N Orleans St, River North · Open Mon 10 AM–4 PM
The restaurant that inspired The Bear on FX. Counter service Italian beef sandwich. Walls covered in photos of famous visitors. Cash is faster (ATM on site). Quick in-and-out, perfect last meal before the airport.
Order: Italian beef, WET, with hot giardiniera. Know your order before reaching the counter. ~$10–12.
