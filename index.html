About Me

Aerospace Engineer

I'm Dinuka Hannadige, an aerospace engineering student at CU Boulder studying Aerospace Engineering Sciences with a minor in Engineering Management. I care about building things that work under real conditions, whether that's a competition aircraft, a heated ski wax tool, or a racing car aerodynamics package.

I'm the Testing Lead on Colorado Design Build Fly (previously Propulsion Engineer), where I direct cross-functional test campaigns for the team's competition aircraft. I'm also an undergraduate researcher in CU Boulder's Summer Program for Undergraduate Research (SPUR), working on human-robot teaming, and I built a probabilistic wastewater systems model in the Civil, Environmental & Architectural Engineering department.

Outside the lab I serve as Director of Operations for Program Council, CU Boulder's student-run events organization, leading a 20+ person team, and as an Engineering Senator in Student Government representing the College of Engineering. I also work as a Peer Advisor in the Program in Exploratory Studies and have done work with BAE Systems' Space and Mission Systems group.
